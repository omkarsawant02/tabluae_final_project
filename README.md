# AI/ML Job Market & Skill Demand Intelligence

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the AI and Machine Learning job market using Tableau.

The objective is to understand:

- Demand for different AI/ML job roles
- Salary variation across job roles
- Most demanded technical skills
- Geographical distribution of AI/ML jobs
- Distribution of remote, hybrid and on-site work models
- Relationship between experience and salary

The analysis is presented through an interactive Tableau dashboard.

---

## Dataset

**Dataset Name:** AI_ML_Job_Market_2025

**Number of Records:** 15,000

### Important Fields

- Job ID
- Job Title
- Salary USD
- Years Experience
- Required Skills
- Company Location
- Education Required
- Employment Type
- Remote Ratio
- Company Name
- Company Size

## Tableau Worksheets

The project contains the following worksheets:

### 1. Job Role Demand
Shows the number of job postings for different AI/ML roles.

### 2. Salary by Role
Compares average salary across different AI/ML job roles.

### 3. Experience vs Salary
Shows the relationship between years of experience and salary.

### 4. Top Skills in Demand
Identifies the most frequently required technical skills.

### 5. Job Location
Shows the distribution of AI/ML job postings across different locations.

### 6. Work Model
Compares on-site, hybrid and remote job opportunities.

---

## Key Insights

- Python is the most frequently demanded skill in the dataset.
- SQL is another highly demanded technical skill.
- Machine Learning and AI engineering-related roles have strong demand in the dataset.
- Average salary varies across different AI/ML job roles.
- Job postings are distributed across multiple countries.
- On-site, hybrid and remote opportunities have relatively similar distributions.
